ABOUT

Cyan Lin is an illustrator based in Beijing, China. Her illustrations focus on narratives inspired by the world around us. Her work centers on women as she draws on her own personal experiences and situation as a female designer. The characters have a traditional modality, involving vintage references, yet the style and colour palette are bright and warm.
